Today, artificial intelligence means the ability of machines to think and learn like humans. It allows computers to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence, such as understanding language, recognizing images, making decisions and solving problems. There should be clear rules about who owns the data and how it is used. In the coming years, AI will become even more powerful with new technologies like machine-learning, deep learning and neural networks. But we must use it wisely and ethically to make the world a better place.
